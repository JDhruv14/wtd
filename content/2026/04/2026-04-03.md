I've been a huge fan of Japanese media since many years now, from watching animes to watching movies now. I've watched many Japanese movies and I watched a movie called _Maboroshi_ directed by Hirokazu Kore-eda. I have watched 4-5 many films made by him and each movie is supreme.

I like his movies because he just knows how to direct movies, how to convery deep messages even when the protagonists are standing still speaking nothing but that silence speaks for itself.

So, it was Sunday and I already had in my list to watch this movie and it's a story about a lday who was newly married and really happy, she wasn't from a big town nor rich but she had a loving husband and newly born child. Life was going good but somehow one day, she was waiting for husband and while waiting she caught a sleep and that's when cops arrive saying her husband is no more, now the thing is cops don't know if it was a suicide or an accident.

She didn't show any feeling on the outside but we can feel her emotions, that incident put a cut in her heart. She has to live with this trauma whole life and she also has a son to raise. We follow her story, how she deals with it and grows eventually.

These are the learnings I feel I learnt from the movie

- Grief is something that everyone has deal with at some point of time and we have to learn to live with it.
- Not all things have reasons, some mysteries remain unsolved.
- Humans are fragile, at the end they need to empathy, love to move on and live a satisfactory life.
- Accepting the situation sometimes can be more healing than finding the root cause of why.

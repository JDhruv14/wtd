So where should we start?

WTD is a project that I started making on I don't exactly remember the date but it was 30/31 March, I had access to Claude Pro for a week, so obviously I wanted to utilize it and I had just shipped my portfolio.

I have always been a keen learner and self introspective guy, I want to document every detail of my life and I believe there are many observations we make everyday but eventually forget about it or the little moments which are lost and also each day brings a new set of challenges and situations which I feel should be saved somewhere so that we can track it. 

I want to maintain a journal of my life to see how I was before and where I have come today after a long journey and that's why I came up with the idea. Yes, this project is about me but I'm sharing it publicly because I feel like in this era of AI and problems we are not talking to our own self, we are clueless about life and just to avoid facing your self we keep on doing stuff just to engage our brain continuously and I am is/was like that and I feel like I have lost my ability to see my own self in the mirror because somewhere deep inside I know I haven't being doing well. So to rectify that mistake I came up with open source project so that we could again join the broken pieces of own self and take a moment to cherish small moments of life away from all the noices, the small things like that smell of freshly brewed coffee, the gorgeous sunset.

I hope that you as reader will get my point even though maybe I'm messing the core message and vision. So, if you want to join feel to reconnect with your own self, do use this as a template no need to showcase it publicly, but do it for your own self.

Also talking about the project development, I made 50-60% of the code in the office using Claude Code web and yes it wasn't the best but I can understand the power of it. I just had a blurry vision for which I used AI to make it into reality.

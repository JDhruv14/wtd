## Design Context

### Users
People browsing a personal daily archive of internet finds, references, films, links, and notes. The site is used as a calm public notebook where the reader can quickly scan dates, discover standout entries, and move through the archive without friction.

### Brand Personality
Internet-native, calm, refined. The interface should feel thoughtful and editorial rather than productized, with enough personality to feel authored instead of templated.

### Aesthetic Direction
Primary reference: Best Thing I've Seen. The UI should keep the intimate archive feel, using quiet hierarchy, sharp typography, restrained glass surfaces, and lightweight chrome.

Secondary reference: Fumadocs interaction discipline for sidebar behavior only.

Anti-reference: dashboard-y SaaS cards, nested card stacks, loud gradients, overbuilt glassmorphism, and generic AI-generated component layouts.

### Design Principles
1. Preserve the feeling of a personal archive before adding UI flourishes.
2. Use glass effects sparingly as atmosphere, not as the main visual idea.
3. Prefer editorial hierarchy over boxed dashboards: typography, spacing, and rhythm should do most of the work.
4. New controls must feel integrated into the existing layout, especially filters and navigation.
5. When the sidebar is fully open, the shell should feel architectural and clean, with no decorative rounded outer edge.
